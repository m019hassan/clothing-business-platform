# File Storage Strategy

## Purpose
Business files should be stored in categories that reflect their purpose, ownership, sensitivity, and long-term value. This helps the business keep information understandable, protected, and easy to review over time.

## File categories

### Product Images
- Business Purpose: Support catalog presentation, merchandising, and customer understanding of products.
- Owner: Catalog team or product managers.
- Retention: Retain for as long as the product remains relevant to selling or reporting.
- Visibility: Generally visible to sales, storefront, and catalog teams.
- Sensitivity: Low to medium; may include brand or merchandising values.
- Future Growth: Likely to grow as product variety and channel coverage expand.

### Product Videos (future)
- Business Purpose: Support richer product presentation and future customer experience needs.
- Owner: Catalog or marketing leadership.
- Retention: Retain while the product remains active or commercially relevant.
- Visibility: Restricted to business teams that need it for catalog and sales support.
- Sensitivity: Low to medium.
- Future Growth: Expected to grow as the business expands its product storytelling.

### Bank Transfer Proofs
- Business Purpose: Support payment verification and financial review.
- Owner: Finance or payments team.
- Retention: Retain for the full period needed for financial review and dispute resolution.
- Visibility: Limited to finance, payments, and authorized management.
- Sensitivity: High.
- Future Growth: Likely to remain important as payment activity increases.

### Customer Attachments
- Business Purpose: Support service requests, account issues, and customer communication history.
- Owner: Customer service or sales operations.
- Retention: Retain for the service period needed to support continuity and review.
- Visibility: Restricted to relevant customer-facing teams and authorized managers.
- Sensitivity: Medium to high depending on the content.
- Future Growth: Likely to grow with customer volume and service complexity.

### Supplier Documents
- Business Purpose: Support procurement, production planning, and vendor relationship management.
- Owner: Operations or procurement leadership.
- Retention: Retain for the period needed for business operations, review, and relationship continuity.
- Visibility: Limited to purchasing, operations, and authorized management.
- Sensitivity: Medium to high.
- Future Growth: Expected to grow as supplier relationships expand.

### Production Documents
- Business Purpose: Support factory coordination, manufacturing readiness, and operational history.
- Owner: Factory operations.
- Retention: Retain while the production context remains relevant and for historical review.
- Visibility: Limited to factory operations and related business teams.
- Sensitivity: Medium.
- Future Growth: Likely to expand with production volume and product complexity.

### Reports
- Business Purpose: Support management review, business oversight, and decision-making.
- Owner: Reporting or business leadership.
- Retention: Retain according to the reporting value and review needs of the business.
- Visibility: Shared with relevant managers and operational leaders.
- Sensitivity: Medium depending on the content.
- Future Growth: Likely to increase as the business needs more insight.

### Generated Exports
- Business Purpose: Support business sharing, review, and operational handoff.
- Owner: The business domain that generates the export.
- Retention: Retain only as long as needed for the purpose it serves.
- Visibility: Limited to relevant users and approved recipients.
- Sensitivity: Medium.
- Future Growth: Likely to increase as reporting and operational sharing expands.

### AI Generated Files
- Business Purpose: Support assisted work, generated summaries, draft documents, and reviewable outputs.
- Owner: The business domain that requested the assistance.
- Retention: Retain while the content remains relevant for business review and audit.
- Visibility: Limited to authorized users and the responsible business area.
- Sensitivity: Medium to high depending on the content and business context.
- Future Growth: Expected to grow as AI-assisted work becomes more common.

### Backup Archives
- Business Purpose: Preserve business continuity and support recovery from loss or disruption.
- Owner: Business administration or operations leadership.
- Retention: Retain for a long business recovery period and according to business continuity policy.
- Visibility: Limited to authorized recovery and governance roles.
- Sensitivity: High.
- Future Growth: Likely to grow as the business stores more data over time.

## File governance expectations
The business should keep files aligned to their purpose and ownership, avoid unnecessary duplication, and preserve the records that matter most to operations, audit, and recovery.
