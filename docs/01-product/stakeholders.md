# Stakeholders

## Business owner
### Goals
- Grow the clothing business sustainably.
- Maintain control over operations, finances, and business risk.

### Expectations
- Clear visibility into sales, inventory, and order health.
- Confidence that sensitive actions are controlled and auditable.

### Risks
- Poor visibility into daily operations.
- Operational errors that affect customer trust or cash flow.

## Employees
### Goals
- Perform daily work quickly and with minimal confusion.
- Access the information and permissions needed for their role.

### Expectations
- Straightforward workflows.
- Clear boundaries around what they can change.

### Risks
- Role confusion.
- Errors caused by missing information or unclear approvals.

## Customers
### Goals
- Buy products easily and receive reliable service.
- Receive timely updates about their orders.

### Expectations
- Accurate stock and order handling.
- Clear payment and delivery communication.

### Risks
- Delayed or incorrect fulfillment.
- Confusion around order status or payments.

## Delivery partners and logistics providers
### Goals
- Receive accurate shipping instructions and delivery information.

### Expectations
- Consistent order and shipment data.

### Risks
- Incorrect addresses or incomplete shipment details.

## Payment providers
### Goals
- Receive verified and properly documented payment activity.

### Expectations
- Clear transaction status and evidence where needed.

### Risks
- Payment fraud, disputes, or unclear verification outcomes.

## Future AI services
### Goals
- Support useful and safe business automation.

### Expectations
- Controlled access to business data and clear approval boundaries.

### Risks
- Misuse, poor data handling, or unsafe action execution.

## Future developers and maintainers
### Goals
- Implement and evolve the platform in a stable and understandable way.

### Expectations
- Clear business requirements and consistent terminology.

### Risks
- Unclear requirements or frequent change without documentation.
