# Product Documentation

This folder contains the business-focused analysis for the clothing business platform. These documents describe what the business needs, how the business operates, and what the software should support over time.

## Document map
- [overview.md](overview.md): business context, problems, users, and expected improvement
- [vision-and-goals.md](vision-and-goals.md): long-term vision, mission, and business objectives
- [business-model.md](business-model.md): how the business creates value across production, inventory, store, and online sales
- [business-processes.md](business-processes.md): major business workflows and operating rhythms
- [business-requirements.md](business-requirements.md): high-level business requirements and priorities
- [functional-requirements.md](functional-requirements.md): features the system should eventually support, grouped by business module
- [non-functional-requirements.md](non-functional-requirements.md): reliability, security, usability, and operating expectations
- [user-personas.md](user-personas.md): the people who will use the system and what they need
- [stakeholders.md](stakeholders.md): the people and organizations affected by the platform
- [user-journeys.md](user-journeys.md): common end-to-end business journeys
- [system-scope.md](system-scope.md): what belongs in the MVP, what is deferred, and what is future-facing
- [mvp-definition.md](mvp-definition.md): a realistic first release for a small clothing business
- [future-roadmap.md](future-roadmap.md): recommended phases after the MVP
- [assumptions.md](assumptions.md): assumptions that shape the product direction
- [constraints.md](constraints.md): business, team, and growth constraints
- [risks.md](risks.md): business risks and mitigation approaches
- [success-metrics.md](success-metrics.md): measurable outcomes for success
- [open-questions.md](open-questions.md): unresolved questions that should be clarified before implementation
- [business-glossary.md](business-glossary.md): the shared vocabulary for all product documentation

## How to use this folder
Read these documents in order when reviewing the business direction. The content is intentionally business-focused and avoids implementation detail so that owners, analysts, developers, and AI agents can align on the same operating model.
