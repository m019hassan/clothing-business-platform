# System Scope

## In scope for the MVP
- Product and variant management for a simple clothing catalog
- Inventory tracking across locations with basic stock movements
- Store and online orders with shared inventory logic
- Payment handling for cash on delivery and bank transfer verification
- Shipping and delivery tracking
- Basic notifications and employee role management
- Simple reporting for inventory, orders, payments, and business activity

## Out of scope for the MVP
- Advanced ERP manufacturing planning
- Advanced analytics and forecasting
- Multi-warehouse optimization
- Multi-currency and multi-language operations
- Fully automated AI workflows
- Advanced loyalty, coupon, and promotion engines
- Deep integrations with many external providers

## Future scope
- Point of sale workflows
- More complete factory planning and production orchestration
- Supplier portals and procurement workflows
- Advanced promotions and customer loyalty programs
- AI assistant support and automation
- Mobile app experiences

## Phase 2
Focus on operational maturity: stronger reporting, richer promotions, more payment and shipping flexibility, and better automation for routine business tasks.

## Phase 3
Focus on expansion: additional sales channels, richer manufacturing capabilities, more advanced analytics, and customer-facing digital experiences.
