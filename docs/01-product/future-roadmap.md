# Future Roadmap

## Phase 2
The next phase should focus on operational maturity and growth support.

### Recommended features
- Point of sale support for physical store operations
- More complete promotions and discount management
- Stronger reporting and simple business dashboards
- Additional payment and shipping flexibility
- Improved notification automation

### Why these come next
These features support higher-volume operations and make the business easier to manage as daily activity increases.

## Phase 3
The third phase should focus on deeper business expansion and more intelligent operations.

### Recommended features
- More advanced manufacturing and production planning
- Supplier and procurement workflows
- Customer loyalty and retention features
- AI assistant support for business questions and workflow assistance
- Mobile application support for staff and customers

### Why these come later
These capabilities depend on the business having a stable core workflow and clear operating discipline.

## Long term
Longer-term investment can focus on broader digital transformation.

### Possible directions
- Multi-branch and multi-warehouse operations
- Multi-currency and multi-language support
- Advanced forecasting and analytics
- Telegram and WhatsApp automation
- Deeper AI support for customer service and internal operations

### Recommendation
The roadmap should remain selective. Not every future capability is necessary, and the business should only invest in expansion once the core workflow is stable and useful.
