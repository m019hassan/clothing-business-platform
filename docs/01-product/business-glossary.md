# Business Glossary

## Product
A sellable item offered by the business.

## Variant
A specific version of a product, such as a size or color combination.

## SKU
A unique identifier used to distinguish a product variant for stock and sales purposes.

## Warehouse
A storage location where inventory is held.

## Inventory
The stock available to the business for sale, fulfillment, or production.

## Reservation
A temporary hold placed on stock for a pending order or cart.

## Order
A customer purchase request that may be pending, confirmed, fulfilled, cancelled, or returned.

## Payment
A financial transaction associated with an order.

## Shipment
The delivery or handoff record for an order.

## Refund
A reversal or reimbursement related to a completed order or return.

## Factory
The production part of the business that creates goods for inventory or customer fulfillment.

## Production order
A planned manufacturing activity that contributes to available inventory.

## Customer
A person or organization that buys from the business.

## Employee
A person who performs work for the business.

## Role
A named grouping of permissions that defines what an employee can do.

## Permission
A specific capability granted to a role or user.

## Notification
A message sent to staff or customers about an important business event.

## Discount
A pricing reduction applied to a product, order, or promotion.

## Coupon
A redeemable promotional code used to offer a discount.

## Promotion
A structured pricing or marketing activity to support sales.

## Store
A physical retail location where the business sells products.

## AI assistant
A future business-facing assistant that can support human users within approved boundaries.
