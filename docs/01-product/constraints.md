# Constraints

## Business constraints
- The business is small and should not adopt unnecessary complexity.
- Growth will likely happen gradually rather than through a sudden scale-up.
- The initial system should be easy to maintain without a large technical team.

## Team constraints
- The implementation team is likely to be small.
- The platform must stay understandable for both operators and future maintainers.

## Operational constraints
- The business needs a practical workflow that fits everyday operations rather than a large enterprise process model.
- The system should support incremental development and avoid a large upfront commitment to advanced capabilities.

## Budget constraints
- The business should prioritize high-value capabilities first.
- Advanced automation, analytics, and many external integrations should be deferred until they are clearly justified.
