# MVP Scope

## Included in MVP
- Authentication and user management
- Roles and permissions with approval-sensitive actions
- Product catalog, categories, and variants
- Inventory with warehouses, stock movements, and reservations
- Customers, cart, checkout, and orders
- Payments for cash on delivery and bank transfer verification
- Shipping and delivery tracking
- Basic notification framework and admin dashboard

## Deferred to Phase 2
- Advanced promotions and discounts
- Advanced reporting and analytics
- Deeper factory planning and BOM support
- Additional payment providers and shipping integrations

## Deferred to future phases
- AI assistant automation
- WhatsApp and Telegram automation
- Mobile application
- Full ERP-style manufacturing workflows

## MVP design principles
- Keep the product catalog simple but extensible.
- Keep inventory rules explicit and consistent.
- Keep approvals focused on high-risk transactions.
- Avoid implementing features that are not needed for the first operational release.
