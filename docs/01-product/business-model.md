# Business Model

## Core business model
The business combines manufacturing, inventory management, retail sales, and ecommerce. The platform is intended to connect these parts so that the business can operate as one coordinated system rather than as separate silos.

## Factory
The factory produces clothing items based on demand, replenishment needs, and customer orders. The business needs a clear view of what is being produced, what is completed, and what remains pending. Factory operations influence inventory availability and order fulfillment.

## Inventory
Inventory is a central business asset. Stock moves between receiving, storage, sale, return, adjustment, and production activities. The business needs accurate and transparent inventory information to avoid stockouts, overselling, and missed orders.

## Physical store
The physical store sells products directly to customers and needs fast lookups, simple checkout, and clear stock visibility. Store staff also need a reliable way to process returns, record payments, and update the business on sales activity.

## Online store
The online store extends the same business operations to customers outside the physical store. Orders placed online still depend on the same inventory, payment, and shipment processes, ensuring that business rules remain consistent.

## Customers
Customers buy products through the store or online. They expect clear product availability, transparent order updates, and reliable delivery or pickup outcomes. Customer trust is affected by the quality of fulfillment and support.

## Employees
Employees perform different roles across sales, inventory, fulfillment, administration, and factory operations. The business needs clear responsibility boundaries and enough visibility for each employee to perform their work effectively.

## Managers
Managers monitor operations, approve sensitive actions, and ensure that daily performance stays aligned with business goals. They need a practical way to review pending issues, stock concerns, payments, and service quality.

## Suppliers
Suppliers provide raw materials, packaging, and other goods used to support production and operations. Reliable supplier coordination affects production planning and inventory continuity.

## Sales channels
The business may sell through a physical store, online storefront, and future channels. The platform should support a common product and inventory foundation so that channels work together rather than competing with each other.

## Future AI assistant
A future AI assistant should support business users by helping them answer operational questions, summarize pending work, and trigger allowed actions within the same business rules and permissions already used by employees.

## Future mobile app
A future mobile experience should support field staff, store activity, and basic business operations without requiring full desktop use. It should be a natural extension of the core business model rather than a separate system.

## How the parts interact
Products are created and priced once, then used across store and online sales. Inventory is updated from receiving, production, and sales events. Orders depend on available stock and payment confirmation. Fulfillment and shipping then convert confirmed orders into completed sales. Managers and owners monitor the overall health of this cycle through reports, approvals, and operational alerts.
