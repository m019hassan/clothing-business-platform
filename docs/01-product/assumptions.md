# Assumptions

## Confirmed
- The business operates as a small clothing business with both physical and online sales needs.
- The business needs a shared view of products, inventory, orders, payments, and fulfillment.
- The initial release should be practical and manageable for a small team.

## Assumed
- The business will use a web-based system for staff and customers.
- Bank transfer verification will be handled through a manual or semi-automated review process in the MVP.
- The business will want a clear approval process for sensitive actions.

## Needs confirmation
- Whether the business wants a fully self-service customer account experience from day one.
- Whether there is a need for multi-location stock management immediately.
- Whether the business already uses a preferred payment provider or shipping vendor.
- Whether the business wants more formal manufacturing workflows in the first release.
