# User Journeys

This document describes complete business workflows from the perspective of key users. Each journey explains what steps the business needs to support without technical implementation details.

## Key Business Journeys

1. **Customer Online Purchase Journey**
- Customer browses available clothing items online
- Selects product(s) and places order
- Chooses payment method (bank transfer, cash on delivery)
- Business verifies payment (if required)
- Order is prepared for shipment
- Customer receives delivery updates
- Order is completed upon delivery

2. **In-Store Sales Journey**
- Customer visits physical store
- Cashier looks up product availability
- Processes sale and collects payment
- Order/receipt is recorded
- Item is removed from inventory
- System updates stock counts

3. **Inventory Management Journey**
- New shipment arrives from supplier
- Inventory specialist receives and verifies items
- Items are stored in appropriate locations
- System stock levels are updated
- Low-stock alerts are triggered when needed
- Periodic counts reconcile physical and system quantities

4. **Payment Verification Journey**
- Bank transfer payment is received
- Employee reviews payment evidence
- Payment is marked as verified or rejected
- Order status updates accordingly
- Customer is notified of status

5. **Return Processing Journey**
- Customer requests return
- Staff verifies eligibility and reason
- Item is returned to inventory (or not)
- Refund process is initiated
- Return is recorded in system

6. **Business Oversight Journey**
- Owner reviews daily operations
- Checks pending orders and payments
- Monitors inventory levels
- Reviews sales reports
- Makes operational decisions
