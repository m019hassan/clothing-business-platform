# User Journeys

## Customer buys online
A customer browses the online store, selects a product variant, places an order, and completes payment. The business then verifies the payment, confirms the order, prepares the shipment, and updates the customer with delivery progress.

## Customer pays by bank transfer
A customer places an order and chooses bank transfer as the payment method. The order enters a pending payment verification state until the business receives and confirms payment evidence. Once accepted, the order can move forward to fulfillment.

## Customer pays cash on delivery
A customer completes an order with cash on delivery. The business must confirm the order and prepare it for delivery, while keeping the payment expectation visible until the order is completed.

## Employee verifies payment
A staff member reviews a pending bank transfer payment, checks the evidence, and marks the payment as verified or rejected. This decision affects whether the order can proceed to fulfillment.

## Employee ships an order
A fulfillment employee prepares the order, records the shipment details, and updates the order's delivery status. The business uses this step to confirm that the order was actually fulfilled.

## Inventory employee receives stock
An inventory employee records incoming stock, assigns it to the correct warehouse, and updates the balance so that the new inventory is visible to the business.

## Manager creates a discount
A manager creates a promotion or discount for a product or group of products. The discount must be clear, controlled, and easy to understand for both staff and customers.

## Owner reviews business health
An owner checks the latest orders, pending payments, low-stock items, and key performance indicators to understand the current state of the business.

## Factory produces goods
A factory supervisor receives production needs, starts the work, and records the completed output so that inventory and fulfillment can reflect the newly available stock.
