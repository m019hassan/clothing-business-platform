# Risks

## Inventory mismatch
### Impact
High

### Likelihood
Medium

### Mitigation
Use clear stock movement rules, reservation handling, and consistent inventory review processes.

## Payment fraud or false payment evidence
### Impact
High

### Likelihood
Medium

### Mitigation
Keep payment verification as a visible business step with approval and audit support.

## Order mistakes and fulfillment errors
### Impact
High

### Likelihood
Medium

### Mitigation
Use clear order status tracking, fulfillment checkpoints, and customer communication.

## Overselling
### Impact
High

### Likelihood
Medium

### Mitigation
Treat available stock as derived from on-hand stock minus reserved stock and enforce this consistently.

## Human error and permission misuse
### Impact
Medium

### Likelihood
Medium

### Mitigation
Limit access by role, keep approvals for sensitive actions, and maintain audit trails.

## Future scaling pressure
### Impact
Medium

### Likelihood
Medium

### Mitigation
Keep the first release simple but structured enough to expand later without a major rewrite.

## Poor documentation and changing requirements
### Impact
Medium

### Likelihood
Medium

### Mitigation
Keep business documentation aligned with the operating model and review it as the business evolves.
