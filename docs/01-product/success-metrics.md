# Success Metrics

Success should be measured in ways that reflect actual business improvement rather than abstract technical performance.

## Operational metrics
- Reduce the time required to process orders.
- Improve inventory accuracy across store and online sales.
- Reduce manual rework caused by order, payment, or shipping issues.

## Financial and customer metrics
- Increase confidence in payment verification and order fulfillment.
- Reduce customer complaints related to stock availability or delayed delivery.
- Support steady sales growth without increasing operational confusion.

## Administrative metrics
- Reduce the time spent on basic reporting and manual reconciliation.
- Improve the visibility of pending tasks, approvals, and operational exceptions.

## Business adoption metrics
- Staff can use the system consistently for day-to-day operations.
- Owners and managers can rely on the system for core operational visibility.
