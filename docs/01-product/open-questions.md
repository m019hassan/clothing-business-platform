# Open Questions

## Minor questions
- Should the business support customer accounts from the first release, or only guest checkout?
- Should product variants be limited to size and color in the MVP, or should the catalog allow more attributes later?
- Should the business use a single default warehouse for the MVP or support multiple locations immediately?

## Important questions
- What is the preferred payment approach for online sales in the first launch: bank transfer only, cash on delivery only, or both?
- How much manufacturing and production planning does the business expect to manage in the first release?
- Does the business need a formal approval workflow for all inventory changes, or only for high-risk actions?

## Critical decisions
- What is the minimum set of business processes that must work reliably before the business can operate the platform in production?
- How much operational complexity is acceptable for the MVP compared with the need for speed and simplicity?
- Which channels are the highest priority for launch: physical store, online store, or both?

## Architecture blockers
- The business should confirm whether there is any requirement for advanced multi-location operations or external integrations before the MVP scope is finalized.
