# MVP Definition

The MVP (Minimum Viable Product) represents the first practical release for a small clothing business to manage daily operations reliably. It focuses on core business workflows that directly impact revenue, customer satisfaction, and operational control.

## What the MVP Delivers

The MVP enables the business to:
- Manage products and inventory across both store and online channels
- Process orders from start to fulfillment
- Verify and track payments (bank transfer, cash on delivery)
- Handle basic shipping and delivery updates
- Control staff access through roles and permissions
- Receive notifications for critical business events
- Generate basic operational reports

## Core Capabilities Included

### Product & Inventory Management
- Create and manage clothing products with variants (size, color)
- Track stock levels across physical store and online storefront
- Adjust inventory for damage, returns, or counting corrections

### Order & Payment Processing
- Accept orders from both in-store and online customers
- Support bank transfer and cash on delivery payment methods
- Verify payments through manual approval workflow
- Track order status from placement to delivery

### Fulfillment & Shipping
- Prepare orders for shipment or in-store pickup
- Record shipping details and delivery status
- Notify customers of order progress

### Administration & Control
- Role-based access (Owner, Manager, Cashier, Inventory Staff)
- Approval workflows for sensitive actions (payment verification, large adjustments)
- Basic audit trail for key business transactions

## What Is Excluded (Intentionally)

The MVP deliberately omits:
- **Advanced manufacturing/production planning** - Not needed for initial operations
- **Complex analytics/dashboards** - Basic reporting suffices for small team
- **Multi-warehouse/multi-branch** - Single-location focus
- **AI automation** - Core workflows must be stable first
- **Advanced promotions/loyalty** - Simple discounts handled manually
- **Customer portal/self-service** - Staff-mediated process acceptable
- **Integration with external payment/shipping providers** - Manual processes work for MVP scale

## Why This MVP Is Realistic

This scope matches a small clothing business that:
- Has 1-2 physical locations plus online sales
- Manages 50-200 active SKUs
- Processes 10-50 orders per day
- Operates with 3-10 staff members
- Needs a system that works immediately without months of setup

The MVP can be operated by existing staff with minimal training and provides immediate value over spreadsheets and manual processes.
