# Domain Modeling

## Purpose of domain modeling
Domain modeling helps the team describe the business in a shared and meaningful way before implementation begins. It makes the platform’s core concerns clearer, supports better decisions, and helps business owners, developers, and AI assistants speak about the same concepts.

## Relationship with product and architecture documents
The product documents define what the business needs. The architecture documents define how the system is shaped to support those needs. The domain documents describe the business reality that both of those layers must respect. Together, they create a consistent foundation for planning and evolution.

## How future domain documents should be organized
Future domain documents should stay focused on business meaning rather than technical design. Each document should cover a specific aspect of the domain, such as an overview, a set of business boundaries, or a shared vocabulary. New documents should be added when a new business concern needs clearer explanation.
