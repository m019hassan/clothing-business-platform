# Domain Overview

## High-level business domain
The business domain is a clothing business operating across multiple channels and business activities. It includes selling products, managing stock, coordinating production, processing orders, handling payments, communicating with staff and customers, and supporting future automation through approved AI assistance.

## Major business areas
The domain is centered on several connected business areas:

- Authentication: ensuring that the right people can access the right business functions.
- Users: the people who work with the platform, including staff and administrators.
- Roles and Permissions: the authority structure that controls what different users can do.
- Products and Categories: the catalog of goods the business sells and the ways those goods are grouped.
- Inventory: the stock held by the business and the movement of that stock across locations and processes.
- Orders: the business requests created when customers buy products.
- Payments: the financial transactions tied to those orders.
- Notifications: the messages that inform people about important business events.
- Factory: the production side of the business that turns materials into finished goods.
- Reports: the business insights needed for operations, planning, and oversight.
- AI Assistant: a future business support capability that helps people work more effectively within approved boundaries.

## How these areas collaborate
These areas work together as a connected operating model. Products and categories define what the business offers, inventory shows what is available, and orders turn demand into completed business activity. Payments and notifications support the financial and communication side of that activity. Factory operations contribute to inventory availability, while reports and permissions help the business stay controlled and informed. The AI assistant, when introduced, should support these business areas without bypassing the rules that govern them.
