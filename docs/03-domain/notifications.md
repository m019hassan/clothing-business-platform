# Notifications

## Notification channels
- In-app notifications
- Email
- Telegram
- WhatsApp
- Future push notifications

## Notification categories
- New order
- Payment pending verification
- Payment approved
- Payment rejected
- Low stock
- Out of stock
- Order shipped
- Order delivered
- Production alert

## Design principles
- Notification rules should be configurable.
- Users and administrators should control their own preferences.
- Notification delivery should be decoupled from business logic through a notification service.

## MVP scope
- Basic notification templates and delivery statuses
- Staff and customer notifications for key events

## Future scope
- Rule-based event routing and provider-specific delivery handling
