# Ubiquitous Language

## Product
- Definition: A sellable item offered by the business.
- Business meaning: A product is the core offer that customers can buy and that the business manages in its catalog.
- Related concepts: Variant, Category, SKU, Finished Product.

## Variant
- Definition: A specific version of a product, such as a size or color variation.
- Business meaning: Variants help the business distinguish how the same product can appear in different forms while remaining part of one product family.
- Related concepts: Product, SKU, Inventory.

## SKU
- Definition: A business identifier used to distinguish a product variant for stock and sales purposes.
- Business meaning: The SKU helps the business track and discuss a specific offering clearly across catalog, stock, and orders.
- Related concepts: Variant, Product, Inventory.

## Inventory
- Definition: The stock held by the business for sale, production, or fulfillment.
- Business meaning: Inventory represents the business’s available supply and the operational reality of what can be sold or used.
- Related concepts: Stock, Reserved Stock, Available Stock, Warehouse.

## Stock
- Definition: The quantity of goods available to the business at a given point in time.
- Business meaning: Stock is the basic measure of supply and is central to sales, production, and replenishment decisions.
- Related concepts: Inventory, Reserved Stock, Available Stock.

## Reserved Stock
- Definition: Stock that has been set aside for a pending order or another business commitment.
- Business meaning: Reserved stock protects the business from overselling and helps maintain confidence in available supply.
- Related concepts: Stock, Available Stock, Order.

## Available Stock
- Definition: Stock that is not reserved and is available for a new business use.
- Business meaning: Available stock is the practical quantity that can support current sales or fulfillment activity.
- Related concepts: Stock, Reserved Stock, Inventory.

## Warehouse
- Definition: A storage location where inventory is held.
- Business meaning: Warehouses help the business organize stock by place and support movement between stores, production, and fulfillment.
- Related concepts: Inventory, Stock, Shipment.

## Raw Material
- Definition: A basic material used in the production process.
- Business meaning: Raw materials are the inputs needed to create finished goods for sale or internal use.
- Related concepts: Factory, Finished Product, Production.

## Finished Product
- Definition: A product that has completed the production process and is ready for sale or distribution.
- Business meaning: Finished products represent the business’s output and the goods that can enter inventory and eventually be sold.
- Related concepts: Product, Inventory, Factory.

## Order
- Definition: A customer purchase request that the business may accept, fulfill, cancel, or return.
- Business meaning: Orders are the central business event that connects customers, products, payments, and fulfillment.
- Related concepts: Order Item, Customer, Shipment, Payment.

## Order Item
- Definition: A specific product or variant included in an order.
- Business meaning: Order items capture the details of what the customer requested and what the business must fulfill.
- Related concepts: Order, Product, Variant, SKU.

## Payment
- Definition: A financial transaction connected to an order.
- Business meaning: Payments represent the financial commitment and status of the sale.
- Related concepts: Order, Shipment, Refund, Dispute.

## Shipment
- Definition: The delivery or handoff record for an order.
- Business meaning: Shipments represent the movement of goods from the business to the customer or another destination.
- Related concepts: Order, Warehouse, Order Item.

## Customer
- Definition: A person or organization that buys from the business.
- Business meaning: Customers are the source of demand and the reason the business exists in the marketplace.
- Related concepts: Order, Payment, Notification.

## Employee
- Definition: A person who performs work for the business.
- Business meaning: Employees are the people who operate the business processes, manage records, and support customers or operations.
- Related concepts: Manager, Role, Permission.

## Manager
- Definition: An employee with authority to oversee operations or approve significant business actions.
- Business meaning: Managers help ensure business rules are followed and that higher-trust actions are handled appropriately.
- Related concepts: Employee, Role, Permission.

## Role
- Definition: A named grouping of permissions that defines what a person can do.
- Business meaning: Roles help the business allocate responsibility in a consistent and controlled way.
- Related concepts: Permission, Employee, Manager.

## Permission
- Definition: A specific capability that allows a person to perform a business action.
- Business meaning: Permissions are the practical expression of business authority and accountability.
- Related concepts: Role, Employee, Approval.

## Notification
- Definition: A message sent to people about an important business event.
- Business meaning: Notifications help keep staff and customers informed about what is happening and what needs attention.
- Related concepts: Order, Payment, Inventory, Customer, Employee.

## Discount
- Definition: A reduction in price applied to a product, order, or promotion.
- Business meaning: Discounts are a business tool for pricing strategy, customer engagement, and sales support.
- Related concepts: Promotion, Order, Product.

## Promotion
- Definition: A structured sales or marketing activity designed to support demand.
- Business meaning: Promotions shape how the business offers value to customers and influence sales behavior.
- Related concepts: Discount, Order, Customer.

## AI Command
- Definition: A request made to an AI assistant to help with a business task.
- Business meaning: AI commands represent the business intent behind assisted work and should be governed by the same rules as any other business action.
- Related concepts: AI Action, Permission, Notification.

## AI Action
- Definition: A step taken by an AI assistant in response to an approved request.
- Business meaning: AI actions should support business goals while remaining accountable, observable, and safe.
- Related concepts: AI Command, Permission, Notification.
