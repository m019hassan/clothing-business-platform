# Quality Attributes

## Maintainability
Maintainability matters because a growing small business needs the platform to remain understandable and manageable over time. The system should be organized in a way that allows new contributors to learn the structure quickly and make changes without creating confusion.

## Scalability
Scalability is important because the platform will likely grow from a small operating footprint to a broader set of users, workflows, and channels. The architecture should support that growth in a controlled way, allowing the business to add capacity and capabilities without a disruptive redesign.

## Reliability
Reliability is essential for the business because operational interruptions can affect sales, production, and customer trust. The platform should be designed to perform consistently and recover gracefully when problems arise.

## Performance
Performance matters because slow experiences can reduce customer satisfaction and create friction in daily operations. The architecture should support responsive interactions for both customers and internal staff, especially as volume increases.

## Security
Security is a core quality goal because business data, financial transactions, and operational records must be protected. The platform should be designed to support controlled access, trusted integrations, and safe handling of sensitive information.

## Testability
Testability is important because a growing business needs confidence that changes do not break existing workflows. The architecture should make it possible to verify critical behavior in a clear and repeatable way.

## Observability
Observability helps the business understand what is happening inside the platform as it grows. The architecture should support clear visibility into health, activity, and issues so that problems can be detected and understood quickly.

## Extensibility
Extensibility ensures the platform can support new business ideas, new channels, and new automation capabilities over time. This quality is important because the business is expected to evolve beyond the initial version, and the architecture should make that growth practical rather than disruptive.
