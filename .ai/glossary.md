# Glossary

## Product
A sellable catalog item representing a core offering in the business.

## Product Variant
A specific variation of a product such as a size-color combination.

## Warehouse
A location where inventory is stored or managed.

## Inventory
The stock position of products or variants across one or more locations.

## Reserved Stock
Stock temporarily held for pending orders or other commitments.

## Available Stock
Stock that is physically on hand and not reserved.

## Order
A customer purchase request that may include multiple items.

## Payment
A financial transaction associated with an order.

## Shipment
The delivery or fulfillment record for an order.

## Customer
A person or entity that purchases from the business.

## Role
A named collection of permissions that can be assigned to a user.

## Permission
An atomic authorization capability such as view, create, update, or approve.
