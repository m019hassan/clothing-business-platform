# Business Rules

## High-level business constraints
- The system must support both store and ecommerce channels through the same core business logic.
- Inventory must be protected from overselling and should be auditable.
- Financial and sensitive operations must be permission-controlled and reviewable.
- The MVP should stay practical and avoid unnecessary complexity.

## Why these rules matter
They keep the platform aligned with business reality and prevent the project from drifting into over-engineered or ambiguous implementation choices.
