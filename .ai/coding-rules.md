# Coding Rules

## General expectations
- Prefer clear, composable, reusable code.
- Keep TypeScript types explicit where they add clarity.
- Keep business logic outside UI components.
- Avoid duplication by extending existing modules and shared utilities.
- Make changes easy to review and explain.

## Why these rules matter
They improve maintainability, reduce hidden complexity, and make the codebase safer for long-term collaboration between humans and AI agents.
